Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

In short
  • Quick Rally has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything you enter stays on your device. The app makes no network requests.
  • We, the developer, never receive or see any of your data.

Who we are

Quick Rally is an app for running Americano and Mexicano sessions in badminton, tennis and padel, made by Ashik Ali Ebrahim Kadher Shahul ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: ashikeks@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The players you add (their names, and gender if you set it for Mixed Americano) and your sessions with their matches and scores. This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and in your device backups. You don't create an account, and we have no way to see it. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Delete all data, or by deleting the app.

Sharing the standings

With Quick Rally Plus you can share a session's standings through the iOS share sheet. The text goes only where you choose to send it (for example Messages or WhatsApp); it doesn't pass through us.

Purchases

Quick Rally Plus is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we receive no payment or personal details, only whether Plus is unlocked on your Apple Account.

Lock Screen

On iPhone you can show the matches on court on your Lock Screen as a Live Activity. It is created on your device and goes nowhere else.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
