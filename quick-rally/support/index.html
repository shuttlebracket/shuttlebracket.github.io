Getting started

  1. Tap Start on the Play tab.
  2. Pick the sport: badminton, tennis or padel.
  3. Type the players' names (or pick them from your players) and tap Start.
  4. Tap Score on a match to enter its score. In tennis you score point by point.
  5. The Play tab shows the rounds and the standings. When the rounds are done, you can play a final for the top four.

Scoring

  • Badminton: rally points to 21, 15 or 11, 2 clear.
  • Padel: a fixed number of points per match; the match ends when they've all been played.
  • Tennis: a set to 4 or 6, 15-30-40 with golden point at 40-40, and a tiebreak at 4-4 or 6-6.
