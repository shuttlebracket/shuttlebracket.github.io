Getting started

The app has a step-by-step User guide: open More → User guide. The short version:

  1. Create a tournament (dates, venue, courts).
  2. Add players, then create events (singles, doubles, mixed, Americano).
  3. In Settings → Draws, add players to each event, set seeds if you like, and tap Generate draw.
  4. Schedule matches, start them and enter scores. Results, standings and rankings update automatically.
  5. Tap Share to create a live link for spectators.

Want to look around first? With an empty app, use Settings → Data → Load demo data.

Common questions

Where is my data stored? Is it backed up?

On your device, and in your normal iPhone/iPad backups. Turn on Settings → iCloud sync to keep the same data on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. Everything works offline. Only live links and iCloud sync need a connection, and they catch up when you're back online.

How do live links work? Who can see them?

A live link shows a read-only, auto-updating view of one tournament in any web browser. Anyone with the link can open it, so share it with the people you want. Email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth and photos are never included. Stop sharing at any time from Share → Stop sharing; links are also removed 90 days after the tournament last changed.

A live link says "Live links are unavailable right now".

The live-link service couldn't be reached. Check your internet connection and try again in a few minutes. If it continues, email us.

How are Americano and Mexicano players ranked?

By matches won, then points. Each win also adds the match's points difference as a bonus (a 21–15 win adds 6). You can change both in Edit event → Americano settings.

I made a mistake in the draw. Can I fix it?

Yes. Regenerate draw lets you keep matches already played (round robin, group stage and Americano) and redraw the rest, or start again. You can also correct any score with Edit score.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings → Data → Reset all data deletes everything on that device. If iCloud sync is on, turn it off first on devices where you want to keep the data. Live-link copies are removed when you stop sharing.

Purchases, restoring and refunds

Any in-app purchases are handled by Apple and tied to your Apple Account, so they carry over to your other devices signed in with the same account (use Restore purchases in the app where offered). For refunds, see reportaproblem.apple.com.
