Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026

In short
  • Shuttle Bracket has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything you enter stays on your iPhone or iPad.
  • Only if you turn them on: iCloud sync keeps a copy in your own iCloud, and a live link publishes a read-only copy of one tournament so others can follow it. Live links never include email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth or photos.
  • If an organiser turns on a registration link, players can sign up through a web form. It asks only for name, gender, club and event (and the partner's for doubles), which go to that organiser.
  • We never sell or share data for advertising.

Who we are

Shuttle Bracket is an app for running badminton tournaments, made by Ashik Ali Ebrahim Kadher Shahul ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: ashikeks@gmail.com.

Information in the app

As an organiser you enter tournaments, events, players and results. For players this can include their name, gender, club, skill level, nationality and, if you choose, an email address, phone number, date of birth and photo.

This information is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage, and in your device backups. You don't create an account, and we have no way to see it. You can delete it at any time with Settings → Data → Reset all data, or by deleting the app.

Because you decide whose details go into the app, please make sure the players are happy for you to record them (and, for junior players, their parent or guardian).

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on Settings → iCloud sync, the app also saves a copy of its data in your own iCloud account so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account can use it. This copy is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we cannot access it. Turn sync off at any time; the data stays on your device.

Live links (optional)

When you create a live link for a tournament, the app uploads a read-only copy of that tournament so that anyone with the link can follow it in a web browser, without an app or account.

What the copy contains

  • Tournament details: name, dates, venue, organiser, description, sponsors, courts.
  • Events, draws, the match schedule, scores and results.
  • For the players in that tournament: name, gender, club, skill level, nationality and ranking points.

What it never contains

Email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth and photos are never uploaded.

Who can see it

Anyone who has the link. Share it only with people you're happy to show the tournament to.

Where it is stored and for how long

The copy is stored with our database provider, Supabase (privacy policy), in London, United Kingdom. It is updated while you keep sharing and is deleted when you choose Stop sharing, or automatically 90 days after the tournament was last changed. Like any web service, the provider may briefly keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the service; we don't use them to identify or track anyone.

Registration links (optional)

An organiser can also share a registration link, which lets players sign up for the tournament's events from a web page.

What the form collects

  • Your name, gender and club (optional), and the event you're signing up for.
  • For doubles, the same details for your partner. Please only enter a partner who has agreed to play with you.

We don't ask for an email address, phone number, date of birth or any other contact details.

What happens to it

Your sign-up is stored with Supabase (in London, United Kingdom) only until the organiser's app collects it, which is usually within minutes, and then deleted from there. It is then kept on the organiser's device as part of the tournament, where the organiser confirms or rejects it. Once the organiser's app has it, your name shows on that tournament's live link, marked as pending or confirmed. Sign-ups the organiser's app never collects are deleted when the organiser stops sharing, or after 90 days at the latest.

To stop abuse, the internet (IP) address of each sign-up is kept for two days and used only to limit how many sign-ups can come from one connection. To change or withdraw a sign-up, contact the organiser.

We process sign-ups because you asked to enter the tournament, and on the organiser's behalf.

The live-link web page

Live links open a page hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub's privacy statement), which may log visitors' IP addresses for security. The page uses no cookies and no analytics. It remembers a viewer's display choices, such as light/dark mode or a followed player, only in that viewer's own browser.

Purchases

Shuttle Bracket Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details or your Apple Account; Apple only tells the app whether Pro has been bought, and the app remembers that on your device.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of information.
  • No accounts or sign-in.

Children

The app is meant for tournament organisers and is not directed at children. Organisers of junior events should get permission from a parent or guardian before recording or sharing a child's details. If you believe a child's details were shared by mistake, contact us with the live link and we'll remove it.

Your choices and rights

  • Data on a device is controlled by the organiser who entered it. Ask them to correct or delete it.
  • Sign-ups through a registration link are handled by the organiser once collected. Ask them to change or remove yours, or contact us about a sign-up that is still waiting to be collected.
  • Live-link copies can be removed by the organiser at any time (Share → Stop sharing). You can also email us the link and we will delete it.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete information about you, to object to its use, and to complain to your data protection authority. We process live-link copies because the organiser asked us to publish them (our legitimate interest in providing the feature they requested). Contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Data on your device is protected by iOS. Live-link copies travel over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and can only be changed by the device that created them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
